How {{ pool.name }} works
1 Group stage — free picks
Tap Home, Draw, or Away on each group-stage match. No bet, no cost.
  • Pick the right side and earn ${{ "%g"|format(scoring_config.group_win_payout) }}.
  • Correctly predict a draw and earn ${{ "%g"|format(scoring_config.group_draw_payout) }}.
  • Wrong pick? Nothing happens to your balance — picks lock 15 minutes before kick-off.
2 Knockouts — wager on the winner
Knockout matches accept real bets. Pick a side, enter a stake, and submit.
  • Your bet is deducted from your balance the moment you confirm.
  • If your side wins, you receive bet × the odds shown next to the team. For example, a $10 bet at 1.8× pays $18.00.
  • If your side loses, you forfeit the stake. ET + penalties always count for knockouts.
  • You can change your bet up until 15 minutes before kick-off; old stakes are refunded.
3 Spying — peek at the field, or one player
Every other player's pick is hidden until kick-off. The 👁 Spy button next to each match opens a single modal with two options:
  • Spy the Field — see the H/D/A vote distribution for ${{ "%g"|format(scoring_config.aggregate_spy_cost) }}, counts only (no names). Once paid you can re-check the live spread any time until kick-off, free.
  • Spy on a player — reveal one specific player's pick. First targeted spy costs ${{ "%g"|format(scoring_config.spy_base_cost) }}; each subsequent one costs ${{ "%g"|format(scoring_config.spy_increment) }} more than the last. Sorted by current balance, highest first.
  • Already-revealed picks stay revealed — free to re-check.
  • After kick-off all picks become visible automatically.
4 Winning
Everyone starts at ${{ "%g"|format(scoring_config.starting_balance) }}. Whoever has the largest balance after the final whistle wins. You can track standings any time on the leaderboard.
5 The payout
Real-money prize pool: number of players × $10 each. When the tournament ends, the in-game balance leaderboard is final.
  • 1st place — wins the rest of the pool
  • 2nd place — wins $40
  • 3rd place — wins $20
